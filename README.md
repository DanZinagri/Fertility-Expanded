# Fertility Expanded
Fertility Expanded is a fertility and family addon aiming to add depth to pregnancy, childcare, and family mechanics.

Every single feature is toggleable

## Features
-Fertility cycles for female pawns
-Menopause and eventual loss of fertility.
-Twins and triplets, an inheritable multi-birth predisposition gene.
-Postpartum recovery, exhaustion, and a chance of postpartum depression.
-Parent-baby bonding, co-parent support, and reactions when a child is harmed.
-Breast milk can be expressed by pawns. with configurable production and capacity by xenotype.
-A lactation implant that maintains milk production without lactation's usual fertility reduction.
-Emergency adoption and foster-placement quests and adoptive and foster family relationships.
-Birth control, herbal contraceptives, fertility treatments, and growable milk thistle and Queen Anne's lace.
-Adjustable vasectomy and reversal behavior, including age-related reversal failure that permits another attempt.

### With Ideology
-New meme.
-Precepts for infant feeding, wet nursing, kinship, communal parenting, postpartum care, and pregnancy termination. Beliefs affect caregiver preference and reactions to family events.
-A Wet Nurse role that maintains lactation and supports infant care.
-New ritual, a quiet welcome ceremony, available for 3 days after birth completes. Twins and triplets can share one ceremony.
